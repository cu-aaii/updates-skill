# Meeting Updates Prompt

Copy everything below the line into Copilot or any AI assistant. Paste your notes at the bottom if you have them, or leave that part empty and let the assistant find your work.

---

Turn my recent work into short, concrete updates I can bring to a team meeting.

**1. Read my notes.** If I pasted notes below, they are the main source. Where they conflict with anything you find, my notes win.

**2. Check my tools anyway.** Notes miss things. Check any of these you can access for the last 7 days (or the period I name): Teams chats and meeting recaps, Outlook email and calendar, SharePoint, OneDrive and Loop files I edited, Jira issues I created, updated or was assigned, and Confluence pages I wrote or edited. Add anything relevant my notes left out and mark it "(found, please confirm)". Only use what you actually find. Never invent work, people, owners or dates.

**3. Ask when it is thin.** If you found nothing, or cannot say for each item what changed, where it stands, and what is next, do not guess. Ask only the questions below that are still unanswered, then stop and wait:

> Rough answers are fine:
> 1. What did you work on since the last meeting?
> 2. What is finished, and what is still in progress?
> 3. Is anything blocked, or do you need a decision or help from someone?
> 4. What is next, and by when?

**4. Write the updates.** Group by project or initiative, using the names the team uses in Jira or Confluence when you can tell. Leave out routine activity like scheduling and FYIs. Keep it plain, specific and short. No em dashes. Use this format:

```
## Updates: <my name>, <start date> to <end date>

**For the agenda:** <items that need team discussion, a decision, or help; or "None">

### <Project or initiative>
<Summary: 1 to 3 sentences on what changed and where it stands.>
Blocker: <only if one exists>
Next step: <specific action>, <owner>, <date if known>
```

Only include Blocker and Next step lines when they exist and the summary does not already say them. End with one line listing the sources you used. Then ask me if anything is missing or wrong.
